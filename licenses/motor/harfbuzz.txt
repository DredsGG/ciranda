ja-runs.txt
===========

Derived from the copyright-expired Japanese novel "Wagahai wa Neko de
Aru" (吾輩は猫である) by Natsume Soseki (夏目漱石), first published in
1905 and 1906.  The source text was prepared and distributed by Aozora
Bunko, which permits free reproduction, redistribution, sharing, and
adaptation of its copyright-expired files:

  https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html

Work and source-file information:

  https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000148/card789.html
  https://www.aozora.gr.jp/cards/000148/files/789_ruby_5639.zip

Downloaded source ZIP SHA-256:

  6545750b89ee2c57f215a65079eeab56ee7c997373b7b86e5b00ae74fe69208f

Source edition: "Natsume Soseki Zenshu 1", Chikuma Bunko, Chikuma
Shobo, first printing dated September 29, 1987.  Text entered by
柴田卓治.  Proofread by 渡部峰子, おのしげひこ, 田尻幹二,
高橋真也, しず, and 瀬戸さえ子.

For this performance corpus, the Shift-JIS source was converted to
UTF-8; its header, footer, ruby readings, and Aozora annotations were
removed; and a 100,000-character sample of Han, Hiragana, Katakana, or
Inherited characters was retained from the beginning of the work.  Each
line is one Unicode-script run.  Common and other scripts are omitted
and act as run boundaries.
